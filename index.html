What is a Pebble Automaton?

A Pebble Automaton is a 1-dimensional cellular automaton where each cell contains a non-negative integer number of pebbles.

At each step, when a cell has $n$ pebbles, it pushes $L(n)$ pebbles to its left neighbor, $R(n)$ pebbles to its right neighbor, and keeps $S(n) = n - L(n) - R(n)$ in place.

Busy Beaver Function: peBBle(n)

peBBle(n) is the maximum number of steps any $n$-pebble automaton takes to stabilize into a static state starting from a single cell containing $n$ pebbles.

📖 Pebble Automaton Quick Guide

Welcome to the Pebble Automaton Lab! Here is how to have the most fun exploring:

  • Set n to whatever you want: Use the stepper or type any $n \ge 1$ in the Rule Editor or Finder.
  • Space-Time Diagram: Zoom with mouse wheel or +/- buttons. Click and drag to pan across the infinite tape.
  • Cell Inspection: Hover your mouse over any cell in the diagram to inspect coordinates, value, and where its pebbles are going!
  • Ultra-Fast Finder: Runs at millions of automata per second using the multi-core C engine. Click "Load into Diagrammer" on any champion found to watch it simulate instantly.
